A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Good questions. Clear answers.

Do I need to know how to code?

No previous programming experience is assumed. The course would explain the essentials as you build. Expect to learn technical concepts, troubleshoot problems, and do the exercises.

Am I buying an app or a course?

The product is the course. You learn the process and build your own app. The project illustration on this page is a teaching example, not software included for sale.

Will my app make money?

There is no earnings guarantee. You will learn to investigate demand, control costs, test pricing, and market a product. Whether people buy depends on the problem, audience, product, and execution.

What tools and additional costs should I expect?

The final tool stack has not been selected. AI tools, a domain, hosting, and payment processing may have separate costs. The course will distinguish essential costs from optional upgrades.

Can I enroll today?

This is a website prototype for reviewing the course concept. Enrollment and payments are not active, and no email addresses are collected.

SAMPLE LESSON · MODULE 2

A problem before a product.

A useful app begins with a specific person struggling with a specific task. Start there before asking AI to build anything.

Try this problem statement

“[A specific audience] struggles to [complete a task] because [current obstacle]. Today they use [workaround], which costs them [time, money, or effort].”

  1. Choose an audience you can actually reach.
  2. Ask three people how they handle the task today.
  3. Look for a repeated problem, not just compliments about your idea.

Your exercise: Write one problem statement and list three people you can interview. Ask about their last real experience, rather than whether they might buy an imagined app.
